# Launch experiment

This is a preparation plan, not an executed campaign or measured result. No ad spend, third-party outreach, users, impressions or income is claimed here.

## First-session proposition

Invite two friends to rebuild three paintings. Merge loose pieces into useful clusters, then hand an existing assembly to a teammate. Show real gameplay and a four-piece handoff in launch material; a gallery screenshot or the museum API alone does not demonstrate that difference.

The public app and invitation are the acquisition path. A recipient opens the actual room link, enters their own display name and receives their own access. Sharing never includes another player's private capability. Solo play remains available without registering or configuring a provider.

## Evidence before expansion

Measure completed rooms, repeat sessions and accepted cluster handoffs from actual gameplay records. Separate controlled acceptance rooms from human use. A created room or successful HTTP request alone is not an engaged user. Test whether people use each other's assembled work rather than finishing three independent puzzles; that is the main product risk. Do not treat an invite copy as a successful referral until its recipient actually joins and plays.

Prepare a short real-play clip and concise gallery images only after the graphical game works. Sharing/publishing to outside communities, messaging people and paid acquisition are separate actions; none has been performed by this plan. Compare actual continuation and completion before buying traffic or expanding the content set.

## Revenue preparation

Use the existing publisher-network account/unit and approved consent configuration for optional placements outside active gameplay. Keep unavailable advertising invisible. This app does not sell sponsor placements, pretend to offer premium functionality or promise payouts.

If repeat room use is observed, evaluate whether people want longer-lived private rooms or larger saved collections. Such an option needs an actual entitlement and recurring workflow before it can be sold; it is currently a hypothesis. Track actual network statements and completed payments separately from estimated potential revenue.
