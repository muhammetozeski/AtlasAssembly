# Atlas Assembly

Rebuild three paintings from a mixed rack. Join neighbouring pieces to free room on your tray, then pass a useful cluster to a friend. Finish alone or share one persistent room with up to three players.

Primary address: https://muhammetozeski-binsite.pages.dev/atlas-assembly/ . Current publication and live acceptance are recorded in the [BinSite delivery record](https://github.com/muhammetozeski/BinSite/blob/main/publication.json) and its [Atlas hosting article](https://github.com/muhammetozeski/BinSite/blob/main/docs/Atlas-Assembly-Hosting.md). Source implementation alone is not a delivered-count claim. Permanent source stays in this folder; generated output belongs in publish/ or artifacts/.

- Graphical solo play with saved progress and mouse, touch or keyboard controls.
- Private shared rooms with individual player access, invitations and recovered requests.
- Whole-cluster offers: the recipient continues the same assembled pieces.
- Explicit solo continuation after absent teammates' trays return, retaining contributions.

The application targets .NET 10 for the browser. See [web distribution](docs/Web-Distribution.md) for publishing, the downloadable web bundle and its real D1/expiry requirements. Players do not need a desktop .NET installation. Optional advertising uses the existing publisher and consent gates; actual demand, ad fill and revenue are unverified. The [launch plan](marketing/Launch-Plan.md) is an unexecuted acquisition experiment.

Read [the product contract](docs/Product-Contract.md) before changing gameplay. [Project knowledge](docs/İçindekiler.md) links implementation contracts and evidence; dated handover records belong in Walkthrough/.

The initial collection includes three actual public-domain images from the Metropolitan Museum of Art: Van Gogh's Wheat Field with Cypresses, Hokusai's The Great Wave and Degas's A Woman Seated beside a Vase of Flowers. Their object metadata, credits, image files, dimensions, hashes and CC0 links are bundled; see [artwork content](docs/Artwork-Content.md). No fabricated players, activity, completion records or revenue claims belong in the product.
